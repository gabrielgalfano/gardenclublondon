Process

  1. Consultation

    The journey begins with an in-depth consultation to understand your requirements, needs and preferences. This includes assessing the space and discussing ideas to create a proposal together with budget and timescale deliverables.

    1 of 5

  2. Design

    Based on the initial consultation, the design team crafts a detailed, bespoke garden design that incorporates your brief. Once the design concept is signed off, all plans are finalised, including selecting materials, plants, and any bespoke features necessary for the project.

    2 of 5

  3. Build

    Our award winning in house team undertake the construction phase with precision and professionalism, bringing the design to life whilst our project management team maintain open communication with the client and any other contractors throughout the process.

    3 of 5

  4. Planting

    We work with the best nurseries and growers to source specially selected plants, trees and shrubs, all planted by our expert team.

    4 of 5

  5. Aftercare & Support

    After the garden is completed, ongoing maintenance and support are offered to ensure the garden thrives and evolves, allowing clients to enjoy their outdoor space without worry.

    5 of 5
